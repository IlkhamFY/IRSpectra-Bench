# Reject-risk: from ~54 toward 10

Reject-risk 10 is the accept band. It is not reachable from what ran
here. A populated same-input comparison board on the n=500 payload,
and a second harness replay, are still missing. There is no paid API
budget. The headline integers stay 227/500, 249/500, wall 204/45/251.

~54 is a judgment about the review, not a metric these scripts print.
The experiments below do not measure a new risk number. They change
which objections are still open.

## What is now in hand

**Thinking-tier split.** Recomputed from the deposits, not copied
from the lock note. Earlier no-thinking 300: **118/300** top-1,
**133/300** recall, generation wall 118/15/167, deposited fverify
sum 114/19/167. Thinking-tier 200: **109/200** top-1, **116/200**
recall, generation wall 109/7/84, fverify 90/26/84. They add to
227 and 249. Detail: `docs/EXP_SPLIT_THINKING.md`.

What it buys: the pool is no longer one undocumented protocol. A
reviewer cannot treat 45.4% as a single harness. What it does not
buy: a second solver, or a reason to replace 227/500 with 109/200.
The thinking cut is also the easier draw (simple top-1 73/97 vs
88/151). Risk stays about **54**. Disclosure is not a comparison
board.

**MAYGEN on a declared 31.** Heavy atoms ≤ 12 only. Generation
finished for 15/31 and the gold key was in all 15. GNN top-1 is
**4/5** on the five sets small enough to rank. Fourteen formulas
timed out at 45s. Sn and Si were rejected. The other 469 compounds
were not run. Detail: `docs/EXP_ENUMERATOR.md`.

What it buys: one offline, same-scorer row, with an honest n.
It shows orderly generation covers the small standard-valence
cases and that a deposited ¹³C ranker does not turn that coverage
into a 500-compound solver. What it does not buy: the external
proposer the earlier note said was required before ≤45 is
defensible. MAYGEN is not IR-Agent, not CASE, and not a replay of
the harness. Do not move the risk into the 40s on the back of n=5.

## What still blocks ~10

1. **Same-input comparison board on n=500.** IR-Agent, NMIRacle,
   Alberts, and commercial CASE cannot be run on the printed peak
   lists in this environment. Exact blockers (input shape, NIST
   copyright, license, API): `docs/EXP_EXTERNAL_BLOCKERS.md`.
   No scores were invented for them. One real external row, on
   these inputs, with this InChIKey-14 scorer, is the step that
   makes ≤45 defensible. It was not taken.
2. **Harness replay.** A second proposer on the same 500, same
   stop rule, no prompt shopping. That needs a model call. No
   budget. The thinking/no-thinking split is not that replay: the
   two slices are different compounds.
3. **The 251 never proposed.** Forward-verify does not repair a
   structure that was not in the candidate list. The enumerator
   does not reach those formulas. Until something proposes on the
   hard 469, the generation wall stays the paper.

~10 needs (1) and (2) actually populated, not described. Tonight
does not do that. The paper-ready addition is the split
(118/300 vs 109/200) and a small enumerator table that must not
be set beside 227/500.
