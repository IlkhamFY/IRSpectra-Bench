\section{Blind protocol and pre-registration}
\label{si:protocol}

\subsection{Solver-facing contract}

Each IRSpectra-Bench item is a four-field JSON object. The solver sees
\emph{only}
\begin{center}
\texttt{\{qid, formula, ir\_bands\_cm-1, h\_nmr, c\_nmr\}}.
\end{center}
No name, SMILES, InChIKey, scaffold, atom-type hint, retrieved spectrum,
licence stamp, or PMC id enters the prompt
(main-text Listing~1, adapted from one IRexp record).
Gold constitution is scored offline by the InChIKey-14 indicator
(\S\ref{si:eqs}).

Difficulty is declared \emph{a priori} by RDKit ring analysis:
\emph{simple} iff $\leq$2 rings, no fused/spiro/bridgehead system,
$\leq$22 heavy atoms; else \emph{complex}.
The released $n{=}300$ bench is 50/50 by design (151/149);
the eligible IRexp corpus is 17.5\% simple / 82.5\% complex.
Reweighting lives on validate-clean $n{=}295$
(\S\ref{si:n300}) and is not a new metric.

These files live in the companion spectro-agent tree, not this postcard

\subsection{What the solver may do}

RDKit formula / parse check only. No web search. No retrieved spectrum.
No thinking tier on the scored $n{=}300$ headline.
The expand-500 deposits used a thinking-tier consumer arm and are labelled
as such wherever they appear --- they are not interchangeable with the
no-thinking headline protocol.

A deposit is JSONL of \texttt{\{qid, candidates: [SMILES,\ldots]\}} under a
stated budget. Headline budget: $\leq 3$ ranked SMILES per \texttt{qid}.
Self-ranking vs forward-verified re-ranking must be labelled; they are not
interchangeable.

\section{Scoring contract (equations used in the main text)}
\label{si:eqs}

These are the same identities as main-text Eqs.~(1)--(7).
They are restated so the SI is readable alone.
\textbf{No new metric is introduced.}

A candidate $\hat y$ matches gold $y^\star$ at constitution iff
\begin{equation}
\label{si:eq:ik14}
s(\hat y, y^\star)
=\mathbf{1}\big[\mathrm{IK}_{14}(\hat y)=\mathrm{IK}_{14}(y^\star)\big].
\end{equation}
$\mathrm{IK}_{14}$ is the first 14 characters of the RDKit InChIKey
(connectivity layer). Full stereo-sensitive InChIKey is reported only on
validate-clean $n{=}295$ (\S\ref{si:n300}).

With ranked pool $\mathcal{C}_i=(c_{i1},\ldots,c_{ik})$, $k\leq 3$,
\begin{align}
\label{si:eq:top1}
\mathrm{top\text{-}1}
&=\frac1n\sum_i s(c_{i1}, y_i^\star),\\
\label{si:eq:recall}
\mathrm{recall}@k
&=\frac1n\sum_i \mathbf{1}\big[\exists\,j\leq k:\; s(c_{ij}, y_i^\star)=1\big],\\
\label{si:eq:prec}
\mathrm{prec.}|\mathrm{recall}
&=\frac{\sum_i s(c_{i1}, y_i^\star)}{\sum_i \mathbf{1}[y_i^\star\in\mathcal{C}_i]}.
\end{align}
These are the three reportable numbers. They factorise as
\begin{equation}
\label{si:eq:factor}
\mathrm{top\text{-}1}
=(\mathrm{recall}@k)\times(\mathrm{prec.}|\mathrm{recall})
\end{equation}
when the same pool is used for both stages --- the rates still have different
denominators and must not be differenced.
Self-ranking $\mathrm{prec.}|\mathrm{recall}$ on $n{=}300$ is $118/133$
(88.7\%). That is \emph{not} forward-verify precision.

Forward-verify re-ranks by symmetric chamfer on $^{13}$C peak sets
$P,Q\subset\mathbb{R}$
\begin{equation}
\label{si:eq:chamfer}
d(P,Q)
=\frac1{|P|}\sum_{p\in P}\min_{q\in Q}|p-q|
+\frac1{|Q|}\sum_{q\in Q}\min_{p\in P}|q-p|,
\end{equation}
where $\hat P(c)$ is predicted \emph{blind} to the observed list $P_{\mathrm{obs}}$.
Instrumented wall only on the locked $n{=}194$ (58/65).
Expansion-106: not run. expand-500 official score is in
\S\ref{si:fverify} (103/230 verify vs 129/230 self; no CIs; not a wall).

A pool estimator on an index set $\mathcal{S}$ is
\begin{equation}
\label{si:eq:pool}
\hat p_{\mathcal{S}}
=\frac{1}{|\mathcal{S}|}\sum_{i\in\mathcal{S}} s(c_{i1}, y_i^\star).
\end{equation}
The ICLR headline uses $\mathcal{S}=\{1,\ldots,300\}$
(194 locked $+$ all 106 Opus, flags included).
Exploratory $\mathcal{S}$ of size 524 / 519 / 530
(\S\ref{si:pools}) do \textbf{not} replace that headline.
expand-500 is a separate $\mathcal{S}$ of size 230 (or clean 224) and is
reported on its own row.

Confidence intervals on $n{=}300$ / $n{=}295$ / $n{=}194$ are bootstrap
95\% (\texttt{score\_main.boot}, seed 0, 2000 resamples).
Paired model differences use McNemar with Holm correction.
\textbf{No CIs} are reported for expand-500 or for the exploratory pools.

\paragraph{Formula-only collapse (control, $n{=}60$).}

Formula $+$ IR $+$ $^1$H $+$ $^{13}$C: 14/60 (23\%) / 19/60 (32\%).
Outcomes perfectly nested (McNemar $p{=}0.001$).
The model is reading the peak lists, not only the formula.

\paragraph{Generate-wide (60-compound arm).}
Ten solver agents, up to six candidates each, pooled then re-ranked:
recall $32\%\to\mathbf{42\%}$, top-1 $23\%\to\mathbf{30\%}$
(McNemar $p{=}0.34$); verification precision falls $84\%\to72\%$.
The training-free ceiling remains recall-limited.
This is a success-for-recall panel, not an accuracy claim.

\paragraph{IRexp-fine-tuned generator (locked slice).}
Pooled with Claude: recall $33.5\%\to54.1\%$, top-1 $28.4\%\to35.1\%$
(McNemar $p{=}0.015$).

task. Training is the one intervention already seen to move
$P(\mathrm{true}\in\mathrm{pool})$.

\paragraph{Battery-electrolyte subset.}
$n{=}46$ literature electrolyte chemistry (not operando): 26\% top-1,
recall-bound --- same regime as the locked slice.

\paragraph{Four-model Claude ladder ($n{=}24$).}
Haiku $\subset$ Sonnet $\subset$ Opus $\subset$ Fable is
capability-sensitive but underpowered for adjacent ranks, with a disclosed
protocol asymmetry. Not a headline.

\paragraph{Expansion difficulty gap.}
+106 is easier for this solver than the locked 194 (59.4\% vs 28.4\% top-1).
That gap is a result, not a reason to hide the 106 or to keep $n{=}194$ as
the headline. Pooling to $n{=}300$ does not erase it (main-text Table~2).
