We submit our manuscript entitled ``\emph{Proposal, Not Ranking, Binds Structure Elucidation from Literature Peak Lists}'' for consideration as a conference paper at \emph{ICLR 2027}.

Frontier models report strong recovery on curated libraries, simulated traces, or single-instrument absorbance. We do not rescore those systems. We ask whether one off-the-shelf closed-book model, given only a molecular formula and literature-reported IR, $^1$H and $^{13}$C NMR peak lists, proposes the correct constitution, and whether ranking is what fails when it does not.

We release \textbf{IRSpectra-Bench} as a frozen roster of 500 problems, an RDKit InChIKey-14 contract, and deposits a mechanical scorer can replay. It is a factorised diagnosis, not a populated board.
On one harness, generation top-1 is 45.4\% (227/500) and recall is 49.8\% (249/500).
The generation wall is 227 exact top-1 / 22 in-pool not top-1 / 251 never proposed.
Corpus-reweighted top-1 on a validate-clean subset is 26.5\%.
Forward verification of the same pools lowers top-1 from 227/500 to 204/500.
The recall gap on a 60-compound arm, not on the $n{=}500$ cohort, appears across four vendor families.
